April is a lovely time to go: it's cherry blossom season, so plan around the crowds.

#### Day 1 · Southern Higashiyama

- **Fushimi Inari** early in the morning, before the tour groups[^1].
- Walk up through **Kiyomizu-dera** to the lanes of Sannenzaka and Ninenzaka.

#### Day 2 · Arashiyama

- The bamboo grove at opening time, then **Tenryū-ji** and its garden.
- A boat ride on the Hozu river if the weather is good.

#### Day 3 · Philosopher's Path

- The canal path is lined with cherry trees, at its best in early April[^2].
- End at **Ginkaku-ji**, the Silver Pavilion.
